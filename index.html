About us

Three friends, one city, and a simple question: where does our food come from?

We're three 25-year-olds living in New York City. Like a lot of people our age, we care about what we eat. We want food that's fresh, healthy, and grown by people we can actually name. In a city of more than eight million people, that turned out to be surprisingly hard to find.

Grocery store labels say "local" and "farm fresh," but they rarely tell you which farm, or how far the food traveled to get here. When we went looking for food sourced directly from farms, we found that great options do exist. There are farms within a few hours of the city delivering produce, meat, eggs, and dairy straight to people's doors. The problem is that almost nobody knows about them.

We used these delivery services ourselves and loved them. But every time we mentioned one to friends, coworkers, or neighbors, the reaction was the same: "Wait, you can do that?" The farms were doing the hard work of growing great food. They just didn't have the time, tools, or reach to get in front of the people who were already looking for them.

That's why we started FarmGuys. We're helping local farms reach more people like us: health-conscious city dwellers and suburban families in their own regional network who want to eat local and know exactly where their food comes from. We bring the marketing and digital know-how. The farms bring the food. Together, we shorten the distance between the field and the kitchen table.
