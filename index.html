About us

A question that started in one city and belongs to every community: where does our food come from?

We're Logan Stone, Ethan Goldman, and Scott Laudenbach, three friends who started FarmGuys in New York City. We care about what we eat. We want food that's fresh, healthy, and grown by people we can actually name. In a city of more than eight million people, that turned out to be surprisingly hard to find.

Grocery store labels say "local" and "farm fresh," but they rarely tell you which farm, or how far the food traveled to get here. When we went looking for food sourced directly from farms, we found that great options do exist. There are farms within a few hours of the city delivering produce, meat, eggs, and dairy straight to people's doors. The problem is that almost nobody knows about them.

Every time we told friends, coworkers, or neighbors about the farms we'd found, the reaction was the same: "Wait, you can do that?" That's why we started FarmGuys. The farms grow great food. We help the people already looking for it find them.

And it isn't just a New York problem. Every community, whether it's a big city, a suburb, or a small town, has farms nearby growing great food and neighbors who would gladly buy from them if they knew where to look. Our goal is to connect every community with its own local farms, so that eating fresh and knowing exactly where your food comes from is normal everywhere, not just a lucky find.

Our mission

Local farms grow some of the healthiest, freshest food around. Many of them still struggle to be seen. Meanwhile, more and more people want to know where their food comes from and are actively searching for clean, local options.
